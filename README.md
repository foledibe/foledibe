## 🟠 The Gist

I build things. Sometimes that means writing Python to characterize aerospace hardware, sometimes it means designing a test fixture in CAD, and sometimes it means literally sewing insulation blankets by hand for spacecraft - yes, that's a real sentence about my actual job. Sometimes it means none of that at all, and I'm just painting, directing a short film, or making dinner for too many people.

I'm a CS graduate from Pace University who never really picked a lane, so I stopped trying to. Give me a spec and a deadline and I'll figure out the rest - with a soldering iron, a needle and thread, or a keyboard, whichever the job calls for.
